## LONG DESCRIPTION

### ABOUT THIS GAME

The market just opened. A pension fund is rebalancing billions into blue chips. A hedge fund is quietly building a short position in your biggest holding. Retail traders are piling into a meme stock that's up 30% today. Somewhere, a CEO just sold $3 million in shares — and nobody noticed yet.

**You did.**

StockSim is a deeply simulated stock market where every price move has a reason, every headline has consequences, and every trade you make ripples through an interconnected financial world. This isn't a stock ticker with buttons. This is a living market — and you're in it.

---

### A MARKET THAT BREATHES

Trade 500+ companies across 12 sectors and 80 subsectors — from tech giants to biotech penny stocks. Every company has a CEO with a personality, a product pipeline, founding story, and rivals. Fundamentals evolve each quarter: revenue grows, margins shift, analysts change their minds.

New companies go public through IPOs. Struggling ones get delisted. The economy cycles through expansion, peaks, contractions, and recoveries — each phase rewarding different strategies.

13 sector ETFs let you trade the broader market. Circuit breakers halt trading when things get out of control. The simulation doesn't sleep.

---

### 120 AI PARTICIPANTS — NOT BACKGROUND NOISE

The market isn't empty. It's filled with named funds, institutions, and traders — each with their own strategy, portfolio, and stress level:

- **Index Funds** rebalance on schedule. Learn the pattern, trade ahead of the flow.
- **Pension Funds** buy the dip with deep pockets — until they don't.
- **Hedge Funds** build short positions, deleverage under stress, and sometimes blow up spectacularly.
- **Activist Short Sellers** publish devastating reports. Were they right last time?
- **Market Makers** provide liquidity — until volatility spikes and they vanish.
- **The Retail Mob** follows hype, chases momentum, and can send any stock parabolic.

You start as a nobody in the retail crowd. Grow your influence, and the market starts watching *you.*

---

### THOUSANDS OF EVENTS WITH REAL DEPTH

Not one-liner headlines — every event is a full briefing:

- **Analyst quotes** from named experts at fictional firms
- **Sector impact maps** showing who wins and who loses
- **Historical parallels** — context from past market crises
- **What to watch next** — upcoming catalysts and follow-ups
- **Branching outcomes** — events unfold over days and weeks, taking different paths based on market conditions

Four event tiers scale with difficulty:
- **Tier 1:** Earnings reports, analyst actions, product launches — daily market rhythm
- **Tier 2:** FDA decisions, hostile takeovers, data breaches — weekly disruptions
- **Tier 3:** Sector crashes, commodity shocks, credit crises — monthly challenges
- **Tier 4:** Black swan events — multi-week story arcs that reshape the entire market

---

### INFORMATION REWARDS ATTENTION

Not everyone gets the news at the same time:

- **Day -5:** An SEC filing shows insider selling. You notice — if you're looking.
- **Day -3:** A rumor surfaces. True or false?
- **Day -1:** An analyst quietly lowers their price target. No headline. Just a number.
- **Day 0:** Breaking news. Now everyone knows.

The player who reads between the lines trades before the crowd. That's not luck — that's edge.

---

### CROSS THE LINE — IF YOU DARE

The StockSim Market Authority is watching. But the rewards for bending the rules are tempting:

- Trade on insider information — but the timing correlation might trigger an investigation
- Pump a small-cap with your market influence — but the pattern is textbook manipulation
- Corner a stock by buying 25% of the float — but you'll draw regulatory attention
- Short a weak company into the ground — but if you're wrong, the squeeze is brutal

Your **suspicion score** rises with every shady move. First a yellow warning. Then an investigation. Then a fine. Then a trading ban. How far will you push it?

---

### PLAY HISTORY'S GREATEST CRISES

15 historical scenarios — each inspired by real market events, with multiple possible outcomes:

- A market-wide crash of 22% in a single day
- A tech bubble with stocks at 100x earnings — when does it pop?
- A banking crisis where institutions collapse one by one
- A pandemic that crashes markets 34% in three weeks, then stages the fastest recovery in history
- A meme stock squeeze — retail vs. the establishment

Each scenario branches based on your decisions and market conditions. No two playthroughs are the same.

### KEY FEATURES

- **500+ companies** with dynamic fundamentals, CEO personalities, and evolving product pipelines
- **120+ AI market participants** across index funds, hedge funds, market makers, and retail — all named, all visible

### BUILT FOR DEPTH

StockSim is not a casual clicker and not a simplified tutorial. It's built for players who want to understand how markets actually work — the mechanics, the psychology, and the consequences.

Whether you play it clean or cross the line, the market will test you.

*All companies, persons, and events in StockSim are entirely fictional. No real financial entities are depicted. No financial advice is provided.*
