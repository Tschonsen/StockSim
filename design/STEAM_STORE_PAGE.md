## LONG DESCRIPTION

### ABOUT THIS GAME

The market just opened. A pension fund is rebalancing billions into blue chips. A hedge fund is quietly building a short position in your biggest holding. Retail traders are piling into a meme stock that's up 30% today. Somewhere, a CEO just sold $3 million in shares — and nobody noticed yet.

**You did.**

StockSim is the stock market simulator that refused to cut corners. 500+ companies with CEOs, product pipelines, and evolving fundamentals. 120 named AI market participants with their own portfolios and stress levels. 12,000+ unique event messages with analyst commentary, sector impact maps, and branching multi-week storylines. A regulatory authority that tracks every suspicious trade you make.

We didn't build a stock ticker with buttons. We built an entire financial world.

---

### 500+ COMPANIES — EACH ONE ALIVE

Every company in StockSim is more than a ticker symbol:

- **A CEO with personality** — visionaries burn cash on R&D, cost-cutters boost margins, empire builders acquire everything in sight. CEOs get fired. New ones change the strategy.
- **A product pipeline** — products launch, grow, mature, and decline. A blockbuster launch sends the stock soaring. A failed product drags it down for quarters.
- **Evolving fundamentals** — revenue, earnings, margins, debt, and employee count change every quarter based on events, management, and market conditions.
- **Analyst coverage** — ratings and price targets shift in response to performance. Not static numbers — living opinions.
- **Rivalries** — companies compete. When one wins a major contract, the competitor's stock drops.

Across **12 sectors, 80+ subsectors, and 13 ETFs**. New companies IPO. Failing ones get delisted. The economy cycles through expansion, peaks, contractions, and recovery — each phase rewarding different strategies.

---

### 120 AI MARKET PARTICIPANTS — WITH NAMES, PORTFOLIOS, AND AGENDAS

This isn't a market where you trade against random noise. It's a market where you trade against *everyone*:

**The Institutions** (they move billions, and you can predict when)
- **Index Funds** rebalance on the first of every month. The pattern is public. The edge is yours — if you're fast enough.
- **Pension Funds** buy dips with bottomless capital. They stabilize crashes — until their quarterly rebalancing dumps a sector overnight.
- **Sovereign Wealth Funds** sit on the sidelines for months, then deploy billions in a single crisis. When they move, the floor is in.

**The Hedge Funds** (they make — and lose — fortunes)
- **Long/Short Funds** build positions over weeks. Watch their filings. When they're wrong, the unwind is violent.
- **Macro Funds** rotate entire sectors based on interest rates and inflation. A single decision moves billions from tech to utilities.
- **Activist Short Sellers** publish research reports that crash stocks 25% in a day. Sometimes they're right. Sometimes it's a hit job. Their reputation follows them.
- **Quant Funds** run algorithms that detect momentum before humans can react. You won't beat them on speed — but you can front-run their patterns.

**The Infrastructure**
- **Market Makers** provide liquidity by quoting bid and ask. When volatility spikes? They vanish. Spreads blow out. Chaos follows.
- **High-Frequency Traders** tighten spreads in calm markets and disappear in storms.

**The Crowd**
- **Retail Traders** chase momentum, panic-sell crashes, and occasionally band together to squeeze a heavily shorted stock into the stratosphere.
- **You** — a retail trader at first. But grow your influence, and the entire market starts reacting to your moves.

Every participant has **visible positions, stress levels, and a track record.** Their trades show up in your news feed. Their buying walls appear in your orderbook. When a hedge fund blows up, you'll know — because you'll see the liquidation cascade in real time.

### 12,000+ EVENTS — NOT HEADLINES. BRIEFINGS.

Most market games give you one-line news: *"Company X beats earnings."* StockSim gives you the full picture:

- **Analyst quotes** from 200+ named experts — *"This is the most significant supply disruption since the last commodity crisis" — Marcus Webb, Atlantic Research*
- **Sector impact maps** — not just "energy up." Energy +6%, Airlines -4%, Defense +3%, Renewables +2%. With reasons.
- **Affected companies** with explanations — *"PetroVault: 40% revenue from EU crude. Direct exposure."*
- **Historical parallels** — *"Similar to the commodity embargo of the 1970s, which caused a 300% price spike over 6 months."*
- **What to watch** — specific catalysts coming in the next 48 hours
- **Branching follow-ups** — each event can lead to 2-4 different outcomes based on market conditions. A trade war can end in a deal, a cold war, or an escalation.

**Four tiers of chaos, scaled by difficulty:**

| Tier | What | Frequency | Impact |
|------|------|-----------|--------|
| **Everyday** | Earnings, analyst actions, product launches | 3-6 per day | ±1-5% |
| **Disruptions** | FDA decisions, takeover bids, fraud allegations | 1-3 per week | ±5-15% |
| **Crises** | Sector crashes, commodity shocks, bank stress | 1-3 per month | ±10-30% |
| **Black Swans** | Multi-week arcs that reshape everything | 0-2 per year | ±20-70% |

### 15 HISTORICAL SCENARIOS — EACH ONE BRANCHES

Play through the most dramatic moments in financial history. Each scenario unfolds over weeks, with **2-4 possible outcomes** determined by market conditions and your decisions:

- A market crash of 22% in a single day — do you buy the dip or wait for the second leg?
- A tech bubble where stocks trade at 100x earnings — it can deflate slowly or collapse overnight
- A banking crisis where institutions fall like dominos — or get bailed out at the last second
- A pandemic that crashes markets 34% in three weeks — V-recovery, L-shaped depression, or something in between?
- A meme stock squeeze — it succeeds, fizzles out, or turns out to be a pump-and-dump trap

**No scenario plays the same way twice.**

### INFORMATION REWARDS THE ATTENTIVE

Not everyone gets the news at the same time:

- **Day -5:** A regulatory filing reveals insider selling. No headline. Just a data point in the company profile — if you bother to check.
- **Day -3:** A rumor circulates. Could be true. Could be noise. The odds are 70/30.
- **Day -1:** An analyst quietly adjusts their price target. No breaking news. Just a number change.
- **Day 0:** The headline drops. Everyone reacts. The stock moves 15%.
- **Day +1:** The deep analysis arrives — sector impacts, historical context, what happens next.

The casual player reacts at Day 0. The attentive player was already positioned at Day -5.

**That gap is where the real game lives.**

### CROSS THE LINE — IF YOU DARE

The StockSim Market Authority monitors every trade. But the dark side of the market is very, very profitable:

- **Insider Trading** — act on early information. Spread it across days and stocks to stay under the radar. Or go all-in and pray.
- **Pump & Dump** — use your market influence to move a small-cap. AI traders follow your lead. Sell into the wave.
- **Spoofing** — place massive fake orders to manipulate the orderbook. Cancel before they fill. The pattern is detectable.
- **Cornering** — accumulate 25% of a stock's float. Control the supply. Force short sellers to cover at your price.
- **Bear Raids** — short a fundamentally weak company and watch stop-losses cascade. If you're wrong, the squeeze will destroy you.

Your **suspicion score** tracks everything. It starts at zero.

- **10+:** The shield in the corner turns yellow. You've been noted.
- **25+:** Ambient news mentions unusual trading activity in your stocks.
- **40+:** Formal warning. Your trades in flagged stocks are delayed.
- **60+:** Investigation opened. 30-60 days of scrutiny.
- **80+:** Fines, trading restrictions, margin ban.
- **95+:** Account freeze. You watch your portfolio lose value while you can't touch it.

The question isn't *if* crime pays. It's *how long.*
